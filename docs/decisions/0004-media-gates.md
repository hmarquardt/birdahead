# ADR 0004 — Human approval and explicit asset rights

Date: 2026-11-15. Status: **superseded** by [ADR 0006](0006-automated-curation.md). The following records the rejected Phase Zero per-asset human-review proposal; it is not current policy.

Acquire candidates under documented rights, preferably CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA with exact versions. Research Grade and automated screening help triage but never approve identification. Review difficult confusion pairs independently. Unknown or disputed identity/rights quarantines the asset.

Keep candidate, rejected, quarantined and approved states separate; preserve review history and derivative provenance. Every production asset needs checked rights, human identification evidence, attribution and a digest. Keep restrictive/unclear assets out of releases. Macaulay assets require explicit file-use permission before storage or derivatives.

Consequence: retrieval coverage exceeds approved teaching coverage. Missing age/plumage/call-type metadata creates real review work that must be measured before expansion.
