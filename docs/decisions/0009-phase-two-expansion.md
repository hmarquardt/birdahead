# ADR 0009 — Expand locally to a 100-species teaching universe

Date: November 15, 2026. Status: implemented within the owner's Phase Two authorization.

Derive a deterministic 100-species teaching universe from the seven existing 2016–2025 county histogram exports. Rank by year-round frequency, current seasonal relevance, approaching 14–28-day rise, recreational/confusion-pair value and media availability; force-retain the 25-species pilot; keep unresolved source names and split/lump ambiguity excluded rather than guessed. The 100-species limit and five-photo/three-recording targets are coverage objectives, not permission to weaken rights or biological gates.

Acquire media through bounded, resumable batches with explicit request, byte, retry and candidate budgets. A checkpoint persists after every downloaded asset. Candidate selection uses source-taxon lineage/support, rights, resolution, contributor diversity, distinct observations and quality/duration filters before transfer. Candidate counts do not become accepted coverage; the existing independent rights/biology/model/technical/deduplication gates remain authoritative. No paid inference service, bulk geographic acquisition or modeled raster is introduced.

Permit verified interval extraction from intermittent real recordings when the license allows derivatives. Record parent digest/ID, interval, model/checkpoint, threshold, recipe version, output digest, attribution and modification notice; re-run BirdNET on the clip; retain the parent. Never synthesize calls or imagery.

Release v2 carries data vintage, native-bin/sample-support semantics, coverage tiers/gaps and priority ranks. Its compatibility key covers taxonomy authority/version and source scope, not additive media/species, so learner progress and active sessions survive compatible releases. Flag reports reconcile into the workbench as one append-only deferral per asset, without consuming owner-review seconds and without becoming biological proof.

Stop at the Phase Two review boundary. Production deployment, paid infrastructure, new county downloads, large EBD/SED or modeled-product acquisition, and commercial-use claims remain separately unauthorized. [Phase Two report](../phase-two.md).
