# ADR 0005 — Bounded validation extension

Date: 2026-11-15. Status: accepted from owner Phase 0.5 authorization.

Phase Zero findings and proposed architecture are accepted. The owner authorizes repository remote verification and commits/pushes; official annual histogram import and deterministic curriculum experiments; authenticated Xeno-canto checks; a local-only review workbench; and a bounded 25-species expansion targeting five photo and three audio candidates per species.

Maintain distinct source-verified, machine-screened, human-approved and expert-verified evidence. Rights and biological identification require separate approval. Preserve review timing and append-only audit events; export only assets that satisfy both gates. Uncertainty and unavailable human review stay explicit.

Production application development and infrastructure deployment remain on hold. Stop at the Phase 0.5 review boundary. Manual histogram acquisition is preferred to processing EBD; access challenges do not authorize bypassing source controls.
