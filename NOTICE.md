# Third-party data and media notices

BirdAhead documentation and tooling do not grant rights to third-party data or media. No media binaries, raw eBird observations, authenticated exports, local review databases or private source captures belong in this repository's published commits.

Derived eBird taxonomy crosswalks and occurrence summaries credit eBird, Cornell Lab of Ornithology, https://ebird.org/. They remain subject to the [eBird Data Access Terms of Use](https://www.birds.cornell.edu/home/ebird-data-access-terms-of-use/) and [API Terms](https://www.birds.cornell.edu/home/ebird-api-terms-of-use/), including noncommercial, attribution and redistribution conditions. Original datasets must be obtained from Cornell, not redistributed from BirdAhead. No Cornell endorsement is implied.

Regional iNaturalist aggregate counts and species selection record the source in experiment evidence. Source observations and each individual photo have independent licenses; no photo rights are inferred from an observation license. See https://www.inaturalist.org/pages/terms.

Wikimedia Commons and Xeno-canto media retain their creators' copyright and file-specific licenses. Candidate metadata, exact rights evidence, attribution and source captures remain in the ignored local workspace. An approved-media export retains per-file authorship, source links, exact licenses, modifications and provenance; it does not grant a blanket license to a collection.

Never assume publicly viewable Macaulay Library media may be downloaded, transformed, rehosted or packaged offline. Obtain explicit permission for the intended behaviors.
