BirdAhead documentation and tooling do not grant rights to third-party data or media. No media binaries, raw eBird observations, authenticated exports, local review databases or private source captures belong in this repository's published commits.

Derived eBird seasonal summaries credit eBird, Cornell Lab of Ornithology, https://ebird.org/. They remain subject to the [eBird Data Access Terms of Use](https://www.birds.cornell.edu/home/ebird-data-access-terms-of-use/) and [API Terms](https://www.birds.cornell.edu/home/ebird-api-terms-of-use/), including noncommercial, attribution and derived-product conditions. Original datasets must be obtained from Cornell, not redistributed from BirdAhead; raw histogram exports are not public. No Cornell endorsement is implied. A derived-product notification is prepared for owner review.

Regional iNaturalist aggregate counts and species selection record the source in experiment evidence. Source observations and each individual photo have independent licenses; no photo rights are inferred from an observation license. Public delivery is limited to assets with an explicit public-noncommercial eligibility decision and retained attribution. See https://www.inaturalist.org/pages/terms.

Wikimedia Commons and Xeno-canto media retain their creators' copyright and file-specific licenses. Candidate metadata, exact rights evidence, attribution and source captures remain in the ignored local workspace. Public delivery keeps per-file authorship, source links, exact licenses, modifications and attribution. CC0/CC BY/CC BY-SA/CC BY-NC/CC BY-NC-SA allow the documented public noncommercial display; NC and SA obligations remain and no commercial clearance is granted.

Never assume publicly viewable Macaulay Library media may be downloaded, transformed, rehosted or packaged offline. Obtain explicit permission for the intended behaviors.
